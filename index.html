Talent Pipeline

Where the next generation comes from

The Netherlands has 14 university-level AI programs producing approximately 3,800 graduates annually. Key institutions include UvA, TU Delft, and TU Eindhoven, with the ICAI Network facilitating 50+ public-private research labs.

Hiring Implications

Recommendations for hiring teams

Based on our analysis, here are key strategies for successfully hiring senior AI talent in the Netherlands.

1

Widen geographic search

Look beyond Amsterdam. Utrecht, Eindhoven, and The Hague offer untapped senior talent at lower competition levels.

2

Offer top-quartile comp for Staff+

Staff+ engineers expect €130K-180K+ total comp. Trading firms set the benchmark—match or justify the gap.

3

Leverage the 30% ruling

The 30% tax ruling remains a powerful tool for international hires. Act before the 2027 reduction to 27%.

4

Target emerging AI specializations

GenAI/LLM specialists are still rare (8-10% of profiles). Early movers can capture this growing segment.

5

Prioritize international recruitment

With only 3,800 domestic graduates, international pipelines are essential to meet demand at scale.

6

Move quickly

73% of employers struggle to fill roles. Speed-to-offer is a competitive advantage in this market.
